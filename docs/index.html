ASTRA · 0.1.2 DESKTOP PREVIEW

Your browser.
Your AI workspace.

A Chromium desktop browser with a ZenMux AI sidebar, screenshots and attachments, translation, media tools and inspectable browser actions.

macOS · Apple SiliconWindows · x64Linux · DEBLinux · RPM

New in 0.1.2: A compact AI workspace with more room for replies. Drag the divider to resize the sidebar; your width is remembered. Streaming replies follow new text, show a waiting indicator, and keep existing output on network timeouts. Active streams refresh the idle timeout. This release also adds bookmarks, local history and cookie controls, WebRTC protection, secure DNS tunneling and strict fingerprint controls with Claude verification exceptions.

Work alongside the web

Streaming Markdown answers in a compact, resizable sidebar, with up to five attachments and webpage captures.

Inspectable actions

Page snapshots, action history, approval prompts and completion review.

Bring your model

Configure and test your ZenMux key and model. Follow your system proxy or choose one manually.

Development preview. macOS Apple Silicon is signed and notarized. Windows x64, Linux DEB and RPM packages are available in Releases. Privacy protections reduce exposure; they do not guarantee anonymity or hide proxy/VPN use. Some features remain incomplete; see release notes for validation limits.
